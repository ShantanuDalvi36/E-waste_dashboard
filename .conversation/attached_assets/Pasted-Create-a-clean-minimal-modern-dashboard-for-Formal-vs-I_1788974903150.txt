### Important

All KPIs and charts must update when filters are changed.

Calculate recovery rate using:

`total recovered / total collected × 100`

Do not simply average the existing recovery-rate column.

Format weights in kg/tonnes, currency in Indian ₹ format, and percentages to one decimal place.

Add a small note:

Keep the dashboard visually simple. Avoid excessive colors, gradients, animations, and unnecessary features.
